# Meko Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Meko ("Meko", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") may process information when you use the Meko Discord bot. Meko provides utility commands, custom embed creation, greetings for new members and server boosts, moderation tools, fun commands, giveaways, and server event logging.

## 2. Information Meko may process

The information processed depends on the features a server enables and how people interact with the Bot. It may include:

- **Discord identifiers:** User IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message IDs, and other identifiers made available through Discord.
- **Commands and interactions:** Commands, command options, button interactions, and other inputs sent to the Bot.
- **Server configuration:** Settings configured by server administrators, including greeting settings, logging settings, and saved embed messages.
- **Custom embed content:** Text, titles, descriptions, fields, links, and other content administrators provide when creating embed messages.
- **Moderation data:** Information needed to carry out or record actions such as bans, kicks, timeouts, and message cleanup.
- **Event and logging data:** Information associated with supported events in categories such as applications, channels, Discord AutoMod, emojis, events, invites, messages, polls, roles, stage, server, stickers, soundboard, threads, users, voice, and webhooks.
- **Giveaway and feature data:** Information needed to run giveaways and operate other enabled features.

Discord determines what information is made available to applications through its platform, API, and permissions. Meko does not necessarily receive or store every type of information listed above. The information processed depends on the feature, configuration, permissions, and implementation.

## 3. How information is used

Information may be processed to:

- Execute commands and respond to interactions.
- Create and send custom embed messages.
- Send configured welcome and server boost greetings.
- Perform moderation actions requested by authorized users.
- Operate giveaways and utility features.
- Produce configured event logs for server staff.
- Maintain server settings and feature configuration.
- Troubleshoot errors, protect the Bot, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.
- Respond to support and privacy requests.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 4. Custom embeds, greetings, and logging

The Messages module lets administrators create custom embed messages. The Greeting module can use those embeds for welcome messages and server boost greetings.

Meko offers configurable logging across the categories listed in Section 2. Depending on the events enabled and the information Discord makes available, logs may include event details and identifiers, and may include message-related information. Server administrators should configure logging channels carefully and inform their members about how those channels are used.

Logs sent to a Discord channel are subject to that server's configuration and permissions. People who can access a logging channel may be able to view the information posted there.

## 5. Sharing and third-party services

Meko relies on Discord's services and API to provide its features. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Meko does not use separate third-party analytics or advertising services. The Bot relies on hosting and database infrastructure to operate and store information. Infrastructure providers may process information as necessary to provide those services.

Information may be disclosed when necessary to protect users or the service, respond to valid legal requests, or address security incidents.

## 6. Storage and retention

Meko stores information as necessary to provide its features and maintain server configuration. This may include greeting settings, custom embed messages, logging configuration, and other feature-related data.

Different types of information may have different retention periods:

- **Data with an expiry period:** Information that has a defined expiry period is deleted according to that expiry where automatic deletion is implemented.
- **Persistent configuration:** Server settings, custom embed messages, and other persistent configuration may remain stored until an administrator deletes them, the relevant feature provides for their removal, or a verified deletion request is processed.
- **Other records:** Information used for moderation, logging, giveaways, or other features may be retained according to the relevant feature's behavior and any applicable expiry settings.

Meko does not apply a single fixed retention period to all stored information. Retention depends on the type of information, the feature involved, and its configured expiry, if any.

Logs already posted in Discord channels may remain available until separately deleted from Discord by someone with the appropriate permissions. Removing Meko from a server does not necessarily remove messages or logs that were previously posted in that server.

## 7. Data access and deletion requests

Users may request access to or deletion of information associated with Meko through the [Meko Support Server](https://discord.gg/3sMGcPZBqB).

Please provide enough information to identify the relevant account, server, or feature. Do not share passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive credentials.

We aim to review and respond to verified requests, and complete eligible deletion requests, within **30 days** of receiving the information needed to process them. If a request requires additional time, we will communicate the delay where appropriate.

Some information may need to be retained for security, legal compliance, or dispute resolution. Deleting information stored by Meko does not necessarily remove messages or logs already posted in Discord. Those may need to be deleted separately by someone with the appropriate permissions.
